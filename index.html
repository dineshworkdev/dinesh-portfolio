Process

A Straightforward, 4-Step Workflow

A transparent and collaborative approach to taking your project from initial concept to a successful launch.

01

Discovery & Requirements

We discuss your project goals, required pages, content needs, and timeline to establish a clear project scope.

What you provide Project brief, business details, and reference preferences.
What I deliver Project scope summary, page outline, and agreed timeline.
02

Layout & Visual Planning

Planning the website structure, sections, content flow, and visual direction required for the project.

What you decide Review and approve the layout structure and section hierarchy.
What I deliver Agreed layout structure and section wireframe outline.
03

Development & Testing

Building the website with clean code, ensuring responsive layouts across representative modern desktop and mobile browsers.

What you do Review a private staging preview link and provide feedback.
What I deliver Functional staging preview with cross-device testing completed.
04

Launch & Handover

Deploying the completed website to your domain, setting up live forms, and handing over full ownership.

What you provide Domain or DNS configuration details for deployment.
What I deliver Live website launch, complete source code, and handover guide.
About Me

Independent Web Developer Focused on Building Practical Websites

Direct collaboration, clean standards-based code, and straightforward digital solutions tailored to business needs.

I am Dinesh M, an independent web developer based in Tamil Nadu, India. I work directly with business owners, founders, and creators to build clean, responsive websites and web interfaces that serve practical business needs.

My approach centers on writing clean, maintainable code using modern web standards. I focus on responsive layouts, thoughtful structure, and testing across representative desktop and mobile browsers to deliver a dependable user experience.
